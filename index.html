How to play

  • The city learns your lane and sets the roadblock where you will be. Stay unpredictable.
  • Steer into cash and letters to collect them. Nothing comes to you by itself.
  • Score is how far you run. Cash is what you grab. Cash unlocks characters and upgrades.
  • Spell the word, finish missions, ride ramps and turbo pads. Ten locations to unlock, new music every run.
  • Lose the bike and you run on foot. A bike key shows up on the road now and then: steer into it to ride again.
